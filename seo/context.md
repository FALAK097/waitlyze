# Waitlyze SEO working context

Updated 2026-11-22 from the Search Console and repository review captured in the SEO handoff. Treat this as a dated snapshot; refresh data before making prioritization decisions.

## Product and audience

Waitlyze is a waitlist platform for founders and marketing teams. Its relevant organic landing pages include `/`, `/templates`, `/compare`, `/alternatives`, `/pricing` (the homepage `#pricing` section), and `/features` (the homepage `#features` section). The application also has six generated competitor comparisons under `/alternatives/[slug]`.

Relevant product capabilities include hosted and embedded waitlist pages, referral sharing, email confirmations and Pro automations, signup analytics, an API, and an MCP server. Avoid claims about customer outcomes, traffic growth, or automatic reward delivery unless there is product evidence.

## Search Console snapshot

The previous review reported a submitted sitemap with 84 URLs and no errors or warnings, last downloaded Nov 20, 2026. The Nov 22 query sample was small: most cited queries had 1–8 impressions. Use the observations to choose pages to inspect, not as statistically reliable demand estimates.

- `/blog/waitlist-landing-page-checklist` appeared around position 11 for `join the waitlist landing page` and around 20.8 for `waitlist landing page examples`.
- `/blog/waitlist-email-templates` appeared around positions 14 and 21 for relevant waitlist email queries, with 27 page impressions in the sample.
- `/blog/referral-waitlist-guide` appeared around position 7.5; `/docs/mcp` around position 9; `/alternatives/launchlist` around position 10.
- URL Inspection previously reported `/pricing`, `/features`, and `/about` as unknown. `/about` is not a current route; product feature and pricing destinations are homepage anchors.

## Working rules

- Prefer real, contextual links and explainable product facts over repeated keyword inserts.
- Keep the reader's intent primary; add a product link only where the product solves the task being discussed.
- Recheck current routes, product limits, and Search Console data before publishing claims or changing page targeting.
- Log shipped changes and evidence in `seo/backlog.md` and `seo/log.md`.
