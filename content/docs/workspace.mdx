Workspace controls live at `/settings?section=profile|team|integrations`. Your current workspace scopes the `/waitlists` list, and switching workspaces changes what you see.

## Roles at a glance

| Capability | Owner | Admin | Member |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| View and edit campaigns and audiences | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Publish, pause, roll back | Yes | Yes | No |
| Send email (templates, broadcasts, automations) | Yes | Yes | No |
| Manage connections (Resend, API keys, domains, webhooks) | Yes | Yes | No |
| Delete campaigns | Yes | Yes | No |
| Manage workspace (invite, remove, change roles) | Yes | Yes, except cannot invite admins | No |
| Delete workspace | Yes | Yes | No |
| View audit log | Yes | Yes | Depends on deployment |

Only owners can invite new **admins**. Admins can invite **members**. Personal workspaces behave like owned workspaces with a single owner.

## Profile

Settings → **Profile** shows your name and sign-in email. Email is managed by your sign-in provider and cannot be edited here. Update your display name and it reflects in audit entries and member lists.

## Members and invitations

Settings → **Members** lists people with access plus pending invitations.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Invite by email and role
Choose **Invite**, enter the address, and pick Member or Admin (owners only for Admin). The app caps at 50 pending invitations per workspace.
</Step>
<Step>
### They accept before expiry
Invitees receive a link to `/accept-invitation/[token]`. Expired or already-used tokens show a safe error without leaking membership.
</Step>
<Step>
### Manage access
Resend, revoke, or change roles from the same panel. Removals take effect immediately; the member's waitlist URLs start returning 404.
</Step>
</Steps>

Invitation state moves through `PENDING` → accepted, or `SEND_FAILED` when mail could not be queued, or `EXPIRED` after the window. Every invite, resend, revoke, and role change writes an audit event.

<Callout title="Secure invites need encryption configured">
If member invites cannot be created, the deployment lacks `WEBHOOK_SECRET_ENCRYPTION_KEY`. Contact your administrator - this is a deployment setting, not a docs fix.
</Callout>

## Integrations

Settings → **Integrations** holds workspace-level connections:

- **Resend** sender connection and test. See [Resend](/docs/resend).
- **API keys** per waitlist with expiry and one-active-key rule. See [API keys](/docs/api-keys).

Legacy `#developers` hashes redirect to this section. Bookmark `?section=integrations` going forward.

## Audit log

Where available, Settings supports `?section=audit` showing the newest 200 events first: who did what, to which resource, and when. Covered actions include waitlist created/published/paused, member invited, and publication changes. If the query fails, the panel offers a retry without losing navigation.

## Account export and workspace delete

- **Account export** (`GET /api/settings/export`, signed in) downloads your profile, memberships, campaigns with templates and recipe versions, keys metadata (never plaintext), integrations status, domains, webhooks, and email templates. Use it before deleting anything.
- **Delete workspace** (`?section=delete`, owners/admins only) permanently removes the workspace and everything in it. Export first - deletion has no undo. Personal workspaces warn before removal.
- **Delete waitlist** lives instead in Waitlist settings → Delete waitlist. Export its CSV first. See [Subscribers](/docs/subscribers).
