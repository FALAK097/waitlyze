The **Page** tab at `/waitlists/[id]/edit` has two anchors: `#page-content` for editing and `#publication-actions` for going live. Anyone with edit access can save drafts; only owners and admins can publish.

## Choose your editor

| Waitlist kind | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| Template snapshot waitlists | `SnapshotPageBuilder` with section editing, desktop/mobile preview, hosted-link and embed copy (published only), and publish/pause/rollback. |
| Legacy styled waitlists | `WaitlistGenerator` with colors, borders, radius, fonts, logo, badge, inputs, share toggles, and social proof controls. |

Both editors save to the same draft revision model. Saved drafts increment `templateRevision` without touching `publishedRevision` until you publish.

## Edit content safely

- **Autosave as draft**: edits create a new draft revision. Visitors keep seeing the last published revision.
- **Preview before publish**: check desktop and mobile layouts, form state, success message, and referral display.
- **One email form**: readiness requires exactly one valid email form. Extra or invalid forms block publication.
- **Branding controls**: button, background, inputs, logo, badge, social proof, Waitlyze badge, and Open Graph title/description/image where available.
- **Logo uploads**: raster images are normalized on upload. SVG, active content, invalid, or oversized files are rejected.

<Callout title="Saved is not live">
Saving creates a draft. Publishing copies that draft revision into the live slot. See [Publishing](/docs/publishing).
</Callout>

## Publication actions in the builder

Published waitlists expose real controls only for a published version:

- **Hosted page** copy for `/w/your-slug`.
- **Embed snippet** with your public waitlist ID.
- **Publish** (draft → published, or pending changes → live), **Pause** (stop intake), **Rollback** (restore previous published revision).

URLs follow the configured local, preview, or production environment. Drafts show no public link or embed worth sharing.

## Permissions and validation

- Editing the `sendEmailsToSubscribers` toggle requires email permission; other fields require edit permission.
- Logo keys must belong to the actor (`actorId-` prefix) to prevent cross-user writes.
- Concurrent privileged changes are protected: unchanged form values do not overwrite them.
