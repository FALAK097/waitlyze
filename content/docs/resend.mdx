## Connect your sender

<Steps>
<Step>
### Verify your domain in Resend
Add and verify your sending domain in the Resend dashboard, then create a sending-only API key.
</Step>
<Step>
### Connect in Waitlyze
In [Settings → Integrations](/settings?section=integrations), choose Resend → Connect. Enter the key and a sender email on your verified domain.
</Step>
<Step>
### Save and test
Save the connection, then send a test email to activate it.
</Step>
</Steps>

The saved secret is encrypted and never returned to the browser. Enter a replacement to change it.

## Test and activate

Save the connection, then send a test email. Tests go to the email on your Waitlyze account. The connection becomes active when the test succeeds; until then, Waitlyze uses the deployment sender.

If a test fails, check your key permissions and verified sender, correct the details, and test again. Disconnecting falls back to the deployment sender.

[Resend domain setup](https://resend.com/docs/dashboard/domains/introduction)
