## Templates and tests

Open Email tools → **Templates**. You manage welcome and offboarding messages; confirmation links use a separate transactional verification email. Saved templates support a subject, preview text, and rich message content.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Edit and save
Change subject and body, then **Save**. Unsaved edits are not used for tests or sends.
</Step>
<Step>
### Send a test to a valid address
A test is a **real email**, not an on-screen preview. It uses the saved template.
</Step>
<Step>
### Check verification delivery health
Open a signup from the Signups tab to see its confirmation state. For an unconfirmed signup, an owner or admin can request a new confirmation email after the resend cooldown. The new link replaces the old one.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout title="Delivery needs a sender and a scheduler">
A saved template alone does not send mail. You need a configured sender and bounce/complaint webhook ([Resend](/docs/resend) or deployment default) **and** a running outbox dispatcher (`OUTBOX_DISPATCH_SECRET` plus an authenticated scheduler calling `/api/internal/email-outbox`). Without the scheduler, broadcasts and automations queue but do not leave.
</Callout>

## Consent and suppression — read once

<Callout type="warn" title="Verification does not mean marketing consent">
Broadcasts and automations require **both** verified email **and** explicit marketing opt-in. Unsubscribe and workspace suppressions (bounces/complaints from provider webhooks, including early races) are checked again right before delivery.
</Callout>

- Unsubscribed addresses are excluded from previews and sends.
- Suppressed addresses stay suppressed until the underlying bounce/complaint is resolved.
- Pausing or cancelling stops queued messages, but messages already handed to the provider may still arrive.
