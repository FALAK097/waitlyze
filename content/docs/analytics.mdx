The **Analytics** tab at `/waitlists/[id]/analytics` requires view access. It answers three questions: are people visiting, are they signing up, and are they verifying?

## Metrics at a glance

| Metric | What it counts | Detail shown |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Visitors | Distinct browsers (impressions) | Total for the range |
| Signups | Signup records created | Of which `N verified` |
| Conversion | `signups / visitors` as percent | `—` when visitors are zero |
| Verification | Verified signups share | Plus `N awaiting verification` |

No activity shows an explicit empty state, not zeros pretending to be insight. If the query fails, you get the error with a **Try again** action.

## Ranges and time zones

- Choose **7, 30, or 90 days**. Other values return `400`.
- The request sends your browser time zone; the header shows the active range dates plus that zone.
- Data is `private, no-store`: it always reflects the current waitlist, never a cached copy.

## Activity over time

The trend panel plots visitors, signups, and verified signups per day. Use the view toggle for:

- **Chart** — area visualization with series focus for overlapping lines.
- **Table** — day-by-day numbers with dates formatted in UTC for auditability.

Switching ranges reloads with a loading skeleton and a screen-reader update (“Updating analytics…”). The table view is the accessible source of truth when colors are ambiguous.

## How to read it

- **High visitors, low conversion**: page or offer needs work. Check [Page builder](/docs/page-builder) and [Signup flow](/docs/signup-flow).
- **High signups, low verification**: verification email or sender needs work. Check [Resend](/docs/resend) and delivery health in [Email tools](/docs/email).
- **Steady verification, flat referrals**: enable sharing and nudge inviters. See [Referrals](/docs/referrals) and [Automations](/docs/automations).

The waitlists list reuses the same conversion math per row, so list and detail agree.
