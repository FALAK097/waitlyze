The **Analytics** tab at `/waitlists/[id]/analytics` requires view access. It answers: are people visiting, signing up, verifying — and is the referral loop turning?

## Metrics at a glance

| Metric | What it counts | Detail shown |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Visitors | Distinct browsers (impressions) | Total for the range |
| Signups | Signup records created | Of which `N verified` |
| Conversion | Impression-linked signups / visitors | Verdict chip (`Excellent` at 20%+, `Strong` at 10%+, `Typical` at 5%+, `Needs work` below) |
| Verification | Verified signups share | Plus `N awaiting verification` |
| Viral K-factor | Eligible referrals per verified signup | Verdict chip (`Self-sustaining` at 1.0+, `Growing` at 0.4+, `Early` at 0.1+, `Dormant` below) |
| Points awarded | Referral-engine points in range | Split into join vs referral credit |

Benchmarks are directional (waitlist median conversion ≈ 11%), not targets. A `—` means the denominator is zero, not zero performance.

No activity shows an explicit empty state, not zeros pretending to be insight. If the query fails, you get the error with a **Try again** action.

## Ranges and time zones

- Choose **7, 30, or 90 days**. Other values return `400`.
- The request sends your browser time zone; the header shows the active range dates plus that zone.
- Data is `private, no-store`: it always reflects the current waitlist, never a cached copy.

## Activity over time

The trend panel plots visitors and signups per day. Use the view toggle for:

- **Chart** — area visualization with series focus for overlapping lines.
- **Table** — day-by-day numbers (visitors, signups, verified) with dates formatted in UTC for auditability.

Switching ranges reloads with a loading skeleton and a screen-reader update (“Updating analytics…”). The table view is the accessible source of truth when colors are ambiguous.

## Referral loop

Three steps in order — **Referred in**, **Verified** (share of referred), **Shared again** (share of verified: referred signups who went on to refer eligible signups) — followed by **Top referrers** ranked by eligible referrals, with points. Below it, **Sources & audience** ranks UTM sources, referrer hosts, countries, and device types by signup share.

## Emails

Recent sent broadcasts with delivered and failed counts per send. Confirmation emails stay out of it; use [Email tools](/docs/email) for templates and tests.

## How to read it

- **High visitors, low conversion**: page or offer needs work. Check [Page builder](/docs/page-builder) and [Signup flow](/docs/signup-flow).
- **High signups, low verification**: verification email or sender needs work. Check [Resend](/docs/resend) and delivery health in [Email tools](/docs/email).
- **Steady verification, flat referrals**: enable sharing and nudge inviters. See [Referrals](/docs/referrals) and [Automations](/docs/automations).
- **K-factor below 0.1 with traffic**: the loop isn't turning — check the Referrals view for where it leaks (unverified invitees vs no resharing).

The waitlists list shows a simpler signups-per-visitor ratio per row; the detail page uses impression-linked conversion, so the two can differ slightly by design.
