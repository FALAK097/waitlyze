---
title: Custom domains
description: Serve a published waitlist from a domain you own.
---

Custom domains let you host your waitlist directly on your own branded domain (such as `launch.yourbrand.com` or `waitlist.startup.io`) instead of the default `waitlyze.com/w/slug` URL.

Your custom domain serves the **exact published revision** of your waitlist, including custom styling, social share previews, and referral links, with automatic SSL and zero maintenance.

<Callout title="Included with Pro">
Custom domains are available on Pro plans. You can connect one custom domain per waitlist. Your hosted Waitlyze link continues to function as a reliable fallback at all times.
</Callout>

## How to connect your domain

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open Custom domain settings
In your dashboard, navigate to **Waitlist settings → Custom domain**. Owners and admins can connect and manage domains; team members can view the current connection status.
</Step>
<Step>
### Enter your subdomain
Type the hostname you want to use, such as `launch.example.com` or `waitlist.example.com`. 

We recommend using a subdomain rather than your apex domain (`example.com`) so your main company website or homepage remains unaffected.
</Step>
<Step>
### Add the DNS records at your registrar
Waitlyze provides two records to add to your DNS provider (e.g., Cloudflare, Namecheap, GoDaddy, AWS Route 53, or Google Cloud DNS):

1. **CNAME record**: Points your subdomain to Waitlyze's edge network so visitors are routed to your waitlist.
2. **TXT verification record**: Confirms domain ownership to ensure only you can connect your domain.
</Step>
<Step>
### Verify and launch
Once added, click **Check DNS**. Edge verification and automatic SSL certificate generation usually complete within a few minutes.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Status guide

Track your custom domain status directly in Waitlyze:

| Status | Meaning | Recommended action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ownership check pending** | The ownership TXT record hasn't been detected yet | Confirm the TXT verification record was saved correctly at your DNS registrar |
| **DNS or certificate setup pending** | Ownership verified; SSL certificate is issuing across global edge networks | Wait a few moments, then click **Check DNS** |
| **Ready** | Fully verified and active with HTTPS | Your branded waitlist is live; share your custom URL |
| **Needs attention** | DNS records were altered or failed renewal | Check your registrar DNS settings and re-verify |

## Best practices

- **Keep your waitlist published**: A custom domain will only accept signups when your waitlist status is **Published**. If your waitlist is in Draft or Paused state, visitors will see the published hold screen.
- **Switching domains**: A waitlist connects to one custom domain at a time. To switch domains, click **Remove domain** and add your new hostname. Removing a domain will not delete your waitlist, its signups, or its data.
- **Subdomain vs. Root domain**: Subdomains (`join.brand.com`, `get.brand.com`) provide the cleanest isolation and easiest DNS configuration without interfering with your primary corporate email or website records.
