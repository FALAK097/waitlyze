title: Custom domains
description: Connect a verified domain to a published waitlist.
---

## Add your domain

Open a waitlist's Settings → Custom domain. Enter the hostname you want to use, such as `join.example.com`. Owners and admins can manage this connection.

<Callout title="Availability depends on deployment setup">
If custom domains are unavailable, the deployment must have the domain provider configured. The Waitlyze hosted page remains available after publication.
</Callout>

## Verify DNS and ownership

<Steps>
<Step>
### Add the exact records shown
Copy the DNS record type, name, and value from the application to your DNS provider. Use the records displayed for your hostname rather than a generic hardcoded target.
</Step>
<Step>
### Check again after propagation
Choose Check again. Status may move through ownership check pending and DNS or certificate setup pending before it becomes Ready.
</Step>
<Step>
### Resolve any remaining issue
If status needs attention, compare your DNS records with the current instructions and recheck. Certificate and DNS propagation may take time.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Serve your published page

The domain must be Ready and the waitlist must be published. Remove the domain connection to stop serving through it; this does not delete the waitlist or its Waitlyze hosted page.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Ownership check pending | The verification record has not been found yet. |
| DNS / certificate setup pending | Ownership passed; DNS or the certificate is still propagating. |
| Ready | The domain serves your published page. |
| Needs attention | Compare your DNS records with the current instructions and recheck. |
