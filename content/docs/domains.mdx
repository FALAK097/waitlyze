---
title: Custom domains
description: Serve a published waitlist from a domain you own.
---

Custom domains serve the **same published revision** as the hosted `/w/slug` page. They do not create a separate copy.

## Add your domain

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open Custom domain
Go to Waitlist settings → **Custom domain**. Owners and admins can manage this connection; members can view status.
</Step>
<Step>
### Enter your hostname
Type the exact hostname, such as `join.example.com`. Subdomains are recommended over apex domains.
</Step>
<Step>
### Copy the DNS records shown
Add the exact type, name, and value from the app to your DNS provider. Do not use a generic hardcoded target — use what the app shows for your hostname.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout title="Availability depends on deployment setup">
If the panel says custom domains are unavailable, the deployment lacks a domain provider (`VERCEL_TOKEN`, `VERCEL_PROJECT_ID`, `APP_ROOT_DOMAIN`). The Waitlyze hosted page still works after publication.
</Callout>

## Verify DNS and ownership

<Steps>
<Step>
### Check again after propagation
Choose **Check again**. DNS and certificates can take minutes to hours.
</Step>
<Step>
### Track status
| Status | Meaning | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ownership check pending | Verification record not found yet | Confirm the verification record at your DNS provider |
| DNS / certificate setup pending | Ownership passed; DNS or cert propagating | Wait, then check again |
| Ready (Active) | Domain serves your published page | Share it |
| Needs attention | Records mismatch or expired check | Compare records with current instructions and recheck |
</Step>
<Step>
### Keep the waitlist published
A Ready domain with a draft or paused waitlist still accepts no signups. See [Publishing](/docs/publishing).
</Step>
</Steps>

## Remove a domain

Removing the connection stops serving through it. It does not delete the waitlist or its Waitlyze hosted page. Overview readiness drops the domain check once removed.
