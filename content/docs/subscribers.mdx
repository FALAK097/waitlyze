The **Subscribers** tab at `/waitlists/[id]/subscribers` requires audience view access. Owners and admins can additionally manage and delete.

## Search and filter

- **Search** by email (up to 120 characters). The list announces result counts politely.
- **Status filters**: all, verified, needs confirmation. Subscriber counts update with the filter.
- **Pagination** with `Showing X–Y of Z` and retry on load failure.
- **Clear filters** returns to the full audience when search or status yields nothing.

If the audience is empty, the tab points you to publish and share the page first.

## Inspect a subscriber

Select a row to see email, verification state, signup date, location (city/country when available), referral counts, and marketing consent state. The detail never exposes API secrets or other workspaces' data.

Verification links expire 24 hours after sending. Unconfirmed subscribers stay in **Needs confirmation** and never receive broadcasts or automations. See [Signup and verification](/docs/signup-flow).

## Review referrals

Switch the view to **Referral review** when [sharing](/docs/referrals) is enabled:

- Lists the latest 50 non-`CLEAR` referrals with inviter, invitee, reason, and resolution.
- Approve eligible verified pairs or keep suspicious ones under review.
- Only `CLEAR` and `APPROVED` referrals count toward milestones, queue position, and analytics.
- If the review query fails, the table tells you and offers a retry without losing the audience list.

## Unsubscribe and suppression

Every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link. Unsubscribing stops broadcasts and automations for that subscriber but keeps the signup record. Unsubscribed and workspace-suppressed addresses are excluded from recipient previews and checked again right before delivery.

<Callout type="warn" title="Verification does not mean marketing consent">
Broadcasts and automations require both a verified email and explicit marketing opt-in. Consent is rechecked at send time.
</Callout>

## Export your audience

<Steps>
<Step>
### Filter first
Set search and status to the slice you want. Export respects the current filter.
</Step>
<Step>
### Choose Export CSV
Select **Export CSV** in the Subscribers toolbar. Large audiences stream as a file download.
</Step>
<Step>
### Keep secrets out
The CSV has one row per subscriber with email, verification status, and signup date. It never includes API secrets.
</Step>
</Steps>

Need your full account data (workspaces, campaigns, templates, recipes)? Use the workspace account export in [Workspace](/docs/workspace).
