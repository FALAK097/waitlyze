## Verify subscriber emails

New signups receive a verification email with a confirmation link. The link expires 24 hours after it is sent. A subscriber counts as verified once they confirm; referral credit and queue position only consider verified subscribers.

If a subscriber never confirms, they stay in the Needs confirmation state and never receive broadcasts or automations.

## Unsubscribe

Every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link. Unsubscribing stops broadcasts and automations for that subscriber but keeps their signup record. Unsubscribed addresses are excluded from recipient previews.

<Callout type="warn" title="Verification does not mean marketing consent">
Broadcasts and automations require both a verified email and explicit marketing opt-in. Unsubscribe and workspace suppressions are checked again right before delivery.
</Callout>

## Export your audience

Open the waitlist's Subscribers tab and choose Export CSV. The download respects your current search and status filter, so filter first when you need a slice of the audience.

The CSV includes one row per subscriber with their email, verification status, and signup date. Exports never include API secrets or other workspaces' data.
