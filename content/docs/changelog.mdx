## v0.9.0

### Analytics, with more signal

A clearer view of how every waitlist is growing.

- Explore signups by source, location, device, and referral activity in a redesigned analytics workspace.
- Compare performance across date ranges and track conversion against useful benchmarks.
- See referral leaders and sharing activity alongside the signup funnel.

<div id="release-v080" className="waitlyze-docs-changelog-release-anchor" />

## v0.8.0

### A fairer referral engine

More ways to reward momentum, with safeguards built in.

- Award points for successful referrals with a transparent activity ledger.
- Protect campaigns with velocity checks and duplicate-signup safeguards.
- Give subscribers a clearer view of their position and referral progress.

<div id="release-v070" className="waitlyze-docs-changelog-release-anchor" />

## v0.7.0

### Email that keeps launches moving

Reach subscribers throughout the launch journey.

- Create branded email campaigns from reusable templates.
- Automate welcome and milestone messages as your list grows.
- Connect Resend or bring your own email provider to deliver from your domain.

<div id="release-v060" className="waitlyze-docs-changelog-release-anchor" />

## v0.6.0

### Integrations and dependable delivery

Connect the tools around your waitlist and understand what happened.

- Send webhook events for important waitlist activity.
- Inspect delivery history and retry failed webhook or email jobs.
- Manage workspace integrations and secrets from a single settings area.

<div id="release-v050" className="waitlyze-docs-changelog-release-anchor" />

## v0.5.0

### A more useful subscriber workspace

Spend less time managing rows and more time learning from them.

- Search, filter, and inspect individual subscriber profiles.
- Import signups from a file and export selected audience data.
- Review verification, referral, location, and acquisition details in context.

<div id="release-v040" className="waitlyze-docs-changelog-release-anchor" />

## v0.4.0

### Know where signups come from

Turn waitlist activity into a useful picture of launch momentum.

- Track impressions, signups, conversion, and referral performance.
- Break down the audience by acquisition source and geography.
- Use date filters to focus analytics on the period that matters.

<div id="release-v030" className="waitlyze-docs-changelog-release-anchor" />

## v0.3.0

### Make the waitlist feel like yours

Give every launch page a home that fits the product behind it.

- Build branded waitlist pages with a visual, no-code designer.
- Start quickly with templates, custom colors, and a reusable brand kit.
- Publish pages on a Waitlyze link or connect a custom domain.

<div id="release-v020" className="waitlyze-docs-changelog-release-anchor" />

## v0.2.0

### Sharing that brings in the next signup

Make every new subscriber part of your launch team.

- Give each subscriber a personal referral link to share.
- Add rankings, rewards, and referral progress to the signup experience.
- Track referrals back to the people and channels that generated them.

<div id="release-v010" className="waitlyze-docs-changelog-release-anchor" />

## v0.1.0

### The first Waitlyze waitlist

A simple foundation for collecting early interest in a launch.

- Create a waitlist and publish a signup page without building infrastructure.
- Collect and manage signups in one workspace.
- Start with the essentials: a shareable page, an audience, and launch momentum.
