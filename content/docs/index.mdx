Publish a waitlist page, share it with the world, and connect signups to your own stack.

## Publish your waitlist

<Steps>
<Step>
### Create a waitlist
Open Waitlists, choose Create waitlist, and name your launch.
</Step>
<Step>
### Design your page
Edit its content in the page builder. Preview desktop and mobile layouts before publishing.
</Step>
<Step>
### Publish and share
Publish the page, then copy the hosted URL or embed snippet from Publish options.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout title="Drafts stay private">
Draft and archived waitlists do not accept signups. Edits to a published page must be published before visitors see them.
</Callout>
