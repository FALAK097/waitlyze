Waitlyze is a waitlist product: create a waitlist in your workspace, design its page, publish it, then follow signups, referrals, email, and integrations from one place.

This guide mirrors the app. Each waitlist has **Overview, Page, Subscribers, Analytics, Emails, and Waitlist settings**. Workspace-wide controls live under **Settings**.

## From signup to launch in 5 steps

<Steps>
<Step>
### Create a waitlist
Go to `/waitlists` → **New waitlist**. It starts as a private **Draft**. See [Waitlists](/docs/waitlists).
</Step>
<Step>
### Design the page
Open the **Page** tab to edit content and branding. See [Page builder](/docs/page-builder).
</Step>
<Step>
### Review readiness and publish
Check **Overview**, then publish from the Page tab. See [Publishing](/docs/publishing).
</Step>
<Step>
### Share the link or embed
Copy the hosted URL or paste the embed snippet on your site. See [Hosted pages and embeds](/docs/embed).
</Step>
<Step>
### Follow growth
Track subscribers, analytics, email, and webhooks. See [Subscribers](/docs/subscribers).
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout title="Drafts stay private">
Draft and paused waitlists do not accept signups. Saved page edits are not live until you publish them. Use [Publishing](/docs/publishing) to go live, pause, or roll back.
</Callout>

## Where things live

Each waitlist has six sections - **Overview, Page, Subscribers, Analytics, Emails, and Waitlist settings** - mapped with routes and permissions on the [Waitlists](/docs/waitlists) page. Workspace controls such as profile, members, and the Resend connection live under **Settings** - see [Workspace and roles](/docs/workspace). Webhooks and notification destinations are scoped to an individual waitlist.
