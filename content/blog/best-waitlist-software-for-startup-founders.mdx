## 1. Waitlyze: Best overall for open-source, flat-priced SaaS & AI launches

[Waitlyze](https://waitlyze.com) combines a visual landing page builder (with 14 free launch templates), embeddable signup forms, verified referral loops, launch analytics, email broadcasts/automations (via your own verified Resend sender), and developer tooling in one workspace.

- **Pricing:** **Free ($0 forever)** for 1 workspace, 1 waitlist, and 100 cumulative signups; **Pro ($15/month or $119/year)** for unlimited waitlists, signups, and team members, custom domains, advanced K-factor analytics, and branding removal.
- **Standout strengths:**
  - **Flat workspace pricing:** A viral launch spike never pushes you into a higher subscriber tier.
  - **Built-in anti-fraud:** Cloudflare Turnstile, IP-velocity limits, and email verification before referrals count.
  - **Developer & AI-native:** Open-source codebase, public Signup API, signed webhooks, Slack/Discord alerts, and an official [MCP server](https://waitlyze.com/docs/mcp) for Claude Code, Cursor, and Codex.
- **Honest trade-off:** Subscriber email requires connecting your own Resend sender, and referral rewards are tracked in a ledger rather than automatically fulfilled.

## 2. LaunchList: Best for ultra-low entry cost

LaunchList focuses on quick pre-launch pages and simple referral leaderboards at a low starting price.

- **Pricing:** Free for up to 100 contacts; paid plans start at **$9/month** and scale by contact volume.
- **Best for:** Solo founders who want the lowest possible monthly starting price and basic referral tracking.

## 3. GetWaitlist: Best for API-first and existing sites

If you already have a custom frontend in Next.js, Framer, or Webflow and only want an embeddable form or API endpoint for queue positions and referrals, GetWaitlist is a lightweight option.

- **Pricing:** Starts at **$15/month** (no permanent free tier; short trial).
- **Best for:** Technical teams embedding a waitlist widget into an existing marketing site.

## 4. Waitlister: Best for no-code landing pages

Waitlister offers a drag-and-drop waitlist page builder, referral leaderboards, and built-in email tools.

- **Pricing:** Free up to 100 subscribers; paid plans start at **$19/month**.
- **Best for:** Non-technical founders who want a hosted no-code page and are comfortable starting at $19/month.

## 5. KickoffLabs: Best for gamified contests and giveaways

KickoffLabs is built around sweepstakes, multi-action point scoring, and contest leaderboards rather than simple early-access queues.

- **Pricing:** Limited free tier; paid plans start at **$19/month** and scale with lead volume.
- **Best for:** Consumer launches and giveaways where social contest actions matter more than SaaS beta access.

## 6. Prefinery: Best for gated B2B beta rollouts

Prefinery specializes in invite-code generation, cohort segmentation, and controlled staged rollouts for larger beta programs.

- **Pricing:** Starts at **$49/month** (no permanent free tier).
- **Best for:** Funded B2B teams managing thousands of applicants across structured beta waves.

## 7. Viral Loops: Best for dedicated referral campaigns

Viral Loops is a referral-marketing platform with templates for pre-launch waitlists, milestone rewards, and ambassador programs.

- **Pricing:** Starts at **$49/month** and scales by subscriber volume.
- **Best for:** Marketing teams running dedicated referral-growth campaigns with higher budgets.

## Best waitlist software under $20/month

If you are bootstrapping and want to keep fixed costs under $20/month:

1. **Waitlyze ($0 Free / $15/mo or $119/yr Pro):** Unlimited signups and team members on Pro, plus Turnstile anti-bot protection, custom domains, and MCP/API access.
2. **LaunchList ($0 Free / $9/mo):** Lowest monthly entry price for small lists under its contact caps.
3. **GetWaitlist ($15/mo):** Good if you already have a site and only need an embeddable widget.
4. **Waitlister ($0 Free / $19/mo):** Solid all-in-one no-code option right under the $20 threshold.
