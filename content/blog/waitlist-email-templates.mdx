Silence is the most common waitlist mistake. These seven templates give you a rhythm that keeps subscribers warm without spamming them. Adapt the voice to your product; keep the structure.

## 1. Welcome email (sent right after confirmation)

**Subject:** You're in. Here's what happens next.

```text
Hi {{name}},

Thanks for joining the waitlist for {{product}}. You're #{{position}} on the list.

Here's what to expect:
- A short update every couple of weeks on what we're building.
- Early access before anyone else when we launch.
- Your personal link if you want to move up the list: {{referral_link}}

If you have a question or a feature you want to see, reply to this email. I read every one.

{{founder_name}}
```

## 2. Progress update (every 2-4 weeks)

**Subject:** What we shipped this month

```text
Hi {{name}},

Quick update on {{product}}.

This month we finished {{milestone}}. The hardest part was {{honest_detail}}, and here's how we solved it: {{one_or_two_sentences}}.

What's next: {{next_milestone}}.

If this sounds useful, forward this to someone who would want in: {{referral_link}}

{{founder_name}}
```

## 3. Sneak peek

**Subject:** A first look at {{product}}

```text
Hi {{name}},

We're not ready to launch, but here's a first look at {{feature}}.

{{screenshot_or_link}}

It does {{one_clear_thing}}. We're still deciding {{open_question}}, so if you have an opinion, reply and tell me.

{{founder_name}}
```

## 4. Feedback ask

**Subject:** One question for waitlist members

```text
Hi {{name}},

We're building {{product}} for {{audience}}. One question before we ship:

{{single_question}}

Reply with a sentence or two. It shapes what we build next.

{{founder_name}}
```

## 5. Countdown (one week out)

**Subject:** Launching {{launch_date}}

```text
Hi {{name}},

{{product}} launches on {{launch_date}}.

As a waitlist member you get {{early_access_detail}}. Your position is #{{position}}, and your referral link is still live if you want to bring friends before launch: {{referral_link}}.

Watch your inbox on launch day for your access link.

{{founder_name}}
```

## 6. Launch day

**Subject:** {{product}} is live

```text
Hi {{name}},

It's live. Get access here: {{access_link}}

Your waitlist position was #{{position}}, and as promised you get {{early_access_detail}}.

Thank you for waiting. If something breaks or confuses you, reply and tell me. I'm online all day.

{{founder_name}}
```

## 7. Post-launch follow-up (one week later)

**Subject:** How's it going?

```text
Hi {{name}},

A week since launch. {{one_honest_stat_or_learning}}.

If you haven't tried it yet, here's the fastest way to start: {{quick_start_link}}. If you have, I'd love to hear what's working and what isn't.

{{founder_name}}
```

## Rules that make these work

- **One action per email.** Pick the single thing you want the reader to do and make it obvious.
- **Carry position and referral link.** Every email is a sharing opportunity.
- **Write like a person.** Founder voice beats marketing voice for a waitlist.
- **Send only when there is news.** A quiet list that emails with real updates outperforms a noisy one.
- **Always include an unsubscribe link.** It is required and it builds trust.
