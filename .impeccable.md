## Design Context

### Users
Indie founders and startup marketing teams preparing product launches. They need to publish a branded waitlist, collect interest, encourage referrals, and understand their audience without building the infrastructure themselves.

### Brand Personality
Precise, optimistic, approachable. The owner values cohesive typography, carefully chosen colors, responsive layouts, and purposeful micro-interactions. Visual direction is delegated to the designer, not a copy of the owner's portfolio.

### Aesthetic Direction
An editorial launch workspace: warm paper, dark ink, restrained burnt orange, generous type, fine rules, and useful product previews. Manrope display with Hanken Grotesk body. Support light and dark themes. Avoid glowing gradients, inflated statistics, fabricated testimonials, and delayed content reveals.

### Design Principles
- Show what the product does before asking for an account.
- One clear conversion path; free access is the current offer, not a forever-free promise.
- Match claims to implemented features. Identify example data.
- Make hover, focus, loading, error, and reduced-motion states first-class.
- Isolate public-page styles from the dashboard. Preserve legal substance; disable refund publication until pricing is introduced.
