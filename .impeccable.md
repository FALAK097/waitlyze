## Design Context

### Users
Indie founders and startup marketing teams preparing product launches. They need to publish a branded waitlist, collect interest, encourage referrals, and understand their audience without building the infrastructure themselves.

### Brand Personality
Precise, optimistic, approachable. The owner values cohesive typography, carefully chosen colors, responsive layouts, and purposeful micro-interactions. Visual direction is delegated to the designer, not a copy of the owner's portfolio.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use Dodo Payments as a color and visual-rhythm reference: near-black ink, white and cool-neutral surfaces, and a bright lime accent reserved for clear primary actions and active states. Keep the authenticated product restrained and quiet, with fine dividers, compact readable typography, and useful data surfaces. Reserve soft pastel gradients and dot texture for the public landing hero; do not carry decorative effects into the dashboard. Maintain both light and dark themes without copying Dodo's logo or brand assets.

### Design Principles
- Keep global navigation to Waitlists and Settings; place work in waitlist tabs and use a Settings section index.
- Use lime sparingly so primary actions stay obvious and status colors keep their own meaning.
- Keep interactions fast and dependable, with visible focus, useful loading/error feedback, and reduced-motion support.
- Match claims to implemented features and identify example data.
- Keep public-page effects scoped to the public experience; preserve legal substance and disable refund publication until pricing exists.
